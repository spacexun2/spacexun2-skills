# Deep Question Bank

Use only the sections relevant to the current pressure test. These are probes, not a checklist. Inspect current evidence first, attach a recommended default to material questions, and group related probes under the decision they inform.

## User And Product Promise

- Who is the first real user or decision maker?
- What change should they experience, beyond receiving a feature or artifact?
- What pain or workaround exists now, how often, and why is this worth solving now?
- What is the current non-negotiable outcome?
- What tempting adjacent goal must not replace it?

## Business And Adoption

Use only for a business, commercial, or adoption bet.

- What would prove repeat use, adoption, willingness to pay, or stakeholder commitment?
- Can a manual or processized version prove demand before automation?
- Which cost, delay, or risk is the product expected to remove?
- What result would cause the user to stop investing in this direction?

## Workflow And State

- What is the loop from input to judgment, output, state change, feedback, and next action?
- Which visible actions must create a real record or durable consequence?
- Which pages are entry, workflow, decision, detail, or history surfaces?
- Where must user context survive navigation, return, or later sessions?
- Which failure would make the experience feel fake even if the UI looks complete?

## Data And Trust

- What are the canonical objects, states, and owners?
- Which values are raw, derived, estimated, generated, fixture, or manually entered?
- Which reads must remain read-only and which user actions authorize writes?
- What must be editable, deletable, recoverable, exportable, or auditable?
- What must be real in the current stage, and what may be explicitly labeled validation/demo behavior?

## AI Judgment

Use only when AI is central.

- What decision, ranking, transformation, recommendation, or generation does AI own?
- What manual judgment does it replace or augment?
- Who consumes the output and what do they do next?
- What happens when the output is wrong, stale, or overconfident?
- What evidence, correction, or outcome improves the next cycle?
- Is AI necessary to prove the value, or only to scale it?

## Full-Effect Delivery

- Which named visible capabilities must all exist before the first full pass is meaningful?
- Which bugs, rough edges, or incomplete internal mechanisms may remain afterward?
- Which safety, cost, credential, or destructive risks still require stopping?
- What evidence proves the whole visible loop exists even if polish remains?
- Which completion words are forbidden while a named capability is missing?

## Evidence And Acceptance

- What is the canonical source of truth or approved prototype?
- What commands, browser paths, records, screenshots, or production responses prove the result?
- What evidence level exists now, and what stronger claim would exceed it?
- What would make the result obviously incomplete or unacceptable?
- Which acceptance decision belongs to the user rather than the agent?

## Blind Spots And Cheap Probes

- Which assumption would change architecture or data ownership if false?
- Which preference is hard to describe but easy to judge from a concrete variant?
- Which repo, provider, workflow, or historical document may contain a hidden constraint?
- Which hypothesis can be tested cheaply and reversibly before implementation?
- Which uncertainty is safe to record as a default, and which requires explicit alignment?
