# Compact Goals

Use this reference only for extra compression, a short copyable Goal, or an explicit character or token limit.

## Compression Order

Preserve, when applicable:

1. The irreducible outcome and non-negotiable named capabilities.
2. Reliable source routing.
3. The valid inherited baseline and Remaining delta for continuation work.
4. Prompt-primary authority and the boundary against unreported scope expansion.
5. Explicit stop effects and the stable completion boundary.
6. Terminal evidence appropriate to the promised result.
7. The required runtime-usage final line.

Remove first:

- background already available in current sources,
- repeated rationale,
- source inventories and historical narration,
- generic engineering advice,
- Plan details and routine validation mechanics,
- ceremonial headings, duplicated constraints, and speculative risks.

## Compact Shapes

- Simple task: one direct paragraph, plus a short completion or stop line only when needed.
- Context-bound task: outcome, governing source, prompt-primary decisions, applicable stops, and completion.
- Continuation task: outcome, inherited baseline, Remaining delta, invalidation rule, and completion.
- Multi-capability task: concise capability names plus source or matrix anchor; do not expand every sub-rule or collapse the outcome.
- Read-only task: mutation boundary and required decision artifact.

## Hard Limits

- Count the final artifact deterministically when the user gives an exact limit.
- Shorten wording before removing contract content.
- Do not make applicable stop conditions, the scope-expansion gate, continuation delta, or required usage footer implicit merely to fit.
- If the contract cannot fit, explain the collision and offer the smallest viable alternative instead of silently dropping a boundary.
- Do not invent a numeric token budget.
