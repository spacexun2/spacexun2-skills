# Global Retrospective

Load this reference only when the user explicitly asks for a panoramic review of the whole development history or overall trajectory.

## Reconstruct Before Judging

Recover the smallest history that still explains the trajectory:

- original objective and source;
- user-approved changes and rejected or pending proposals;
- consequential agent recommendations;
- major planning and implementation decisions;
- observed milestones, failures, corrections, and user feedback;
- current product, repository, runtime, and delivery evidence.

Prefer contemporaneous artifacts over later summaries. Distinguish what was believed at the time from what current evidence now shows. Do not infer a missing event merely because it would make the story coherent.

## Inspect The Trajectory

Use `pattern-index.md` as an experience-guided scan, not a checklist requiring a verdict on every entry. Follow candidate signals into their original sources and territory evidence before reporting them.

After the known-pattern pass, inspect whether any material discontinuity, repeated correction, unexpected success, or valuable practice is not explained by the library. Surface a provisional new pattern only when the evidence and future reuse value justify it.

Do not require a first divergence when the history does not support one clear origin. Several independent decisions or changing external facts may explain the current state better.

## Recommend From The Whole

Report only findings that materially change the current understanding or next decision. Preserve useful practices as well as correcting harmful drift.

When several corrections compete, use `roi.md` for the choices whose benefits, costs, opportunity costs, or timing materially affect priority. Do not equate problem severity with investment value.

Let the response follow the evidence: it may be a short narrative, a decision brief, or a longer retrospective. Do not print every pattern that was checked and not found.
