# Pattern Index

Use this compact index for a panoramic retrospective or when the correct detailed pattern family is unclear. Patterns are experienced priors: search for their signals, then confirm or weaken candidates with current evidence.

## Product And Outcome

- **Minimum-loop compression**: a broader intended effect becomes the smallest executable loop.
- **MVP laundering**: MVP, P0, proof, demo, or thin-slice language silently becomes the delivery ceiling.
- **Literal obedience**: wording is satisfied while product meaning or foreseeable design needs are ignored.
- **Goal shrink**: an approved capability, quality bar, fidelity requirement, or acceptance condition disappears.
- **False completion**: validation, deployment, docs, or blocker repair substitutes for the product outcome.
- **Layer omission**: a required visible, logic, state, record, trust, feedback, or next-action layer is absent.
- **Hard-coded intelligence**: final-looking conclusions replace the promised program chain or judgment.
- **Fidelity collapse**: an approved prototype, prompt, contract, or source is treated as loose inspiration.
- **Trust-gap roughness**: a required user-control or safety capability is dismissed as polish.

Load `product-patterns.md` when one of these signals needs deeper judgment.

## Engineering And Process

- **Architecture inflation**: generalized layers are built for hypothetical variants.
- **Harness displacement**: orchestration, validation, or reliability infrastructure becomes the work while the product remains unfinished.
- **Defensive sprawl**: fallbacks, retries, adapters, gates, or state machines protect no current need or observed failure.
- **Source duplication**: an existing prompt, rule, schema, or workflow is recreated as another source of truth.
- **Premature extensibility**: future providers, tenants, plugins, or migrations are supported without a current requirement.
- **Evidence bureaucracy**: matrices and checkpoints exceed what the decision requires.
- **Blocker capture**: a blocker is fixed and its surrounding infrastructure becomes the new target.

Load `engineering-patterns.md` when one of these signals needs deeper judgment.

## Collaboration And Initiative

- **Insight suppression**: a consequential finding or opportunity is not surfaced.
- **User-dependency drift**: shallow delivery repeatedly requires user-led redesign.
- **Recommendation avoidance**: options are returned without an independent recommendation and tradeoff.
- **Question dumping**: ordinary local decisions are pushed back to the user.
- **Approved insight loss**: an accepted proposal never enters the governing target or execution.
- **Silent expansion**: a material idea is implemented before alignment.

Load `collaboration-patterns.md` when one of these signals needs deeper judgment.

## Open Pattern Pass

Do not treat this index as complete. When evidence reveals a consequential recurring structure not explained here, describe a provisional pattern, distinguish it from existing entries, and explain what future decision it could improve. Do not invent a new pattern merely to make the retrospective novel.
